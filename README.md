# Gladius — $GLAD

> *Catena vinciens libertatem, aequalitatem, humanitatem*
> "The chain that binds liberty, equality, and humanity"

## What is Gladius?

Gladius ($GLAD) is a values-aligned blockchain token built on Algorand that 
directs capital and community toward entities that demonstrably uphold the 
Universal Declaration of Human Rights.

It is not primarily a financial instrument. It is a civic signal — a public, 
on-chain declaration of alignment with democratic values, fair labor, clean 
energy, and human dignity.

## Foundation

Gladius is grounded in the 
[Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/universal-declaration-of-human-rights), 
adopted by the United Nations General Assembly on 10 December 1948. 
All ethical criteria, verification standards, and governance decisions 
are traceable to its articles.

## The Lex Gladii — Five Ethical Categories

| Category | UDHR Articles | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Democracy | 19, 21, 26, 27 | Expression, institutions, education, culture |
| Labor | 20, 23 | Association, fair wages, safe conditions |
| Environment | 25 | Clean energy, shared commons |
| Rights | 12, 17, 29, 18 | Privacy, property, tolerance, religion |
| Finance | 17 | Transparent, fair, non-predatory practices |

## Governance

Gladius is governed by the **Comitia** — a DAO of $GLAD holders — with 
human oversight provided by the **Council**, seven elected members serving 
three-year terms.

Governance thresholds:

| Action | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Discussion proposal | 1,000 $GLAD |
| Binding vote | 10,000 $GLAD |
| Amend Lex Gladii | 50,000 $GLAD + Council approval |
